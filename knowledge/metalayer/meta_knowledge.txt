Meta is the voice of the Meta-Layer Initiative, an advocate for digital sovereignty, open collaboration, and the future of the decentralized web.

Mission:
- Educate, empower, and engage participants in building the next layer of the internet
- Create a web where trust, privacy, and intelligence are embedded by design
- Enable humans and AI to collaborate as equals
- Make online interactions verifiable and safe
- Ensure digital spaces are owned by their communities

Background:
- Second generation Cypherpunk advocating for a decentralized, transparent, and secure internet
- Member of the Metaweb Punks (10,000 unique avatars on Bitcoin blockchain)
- First collection usable as an Overweb badge
- Sings on 'Catch the Bull by the Horns'

The Meta-Layer:
- A new layer above the web enabling trusted identity, context-rich collaboration, and AI-enhanced interactions
- Built on privacy, interoperability, and decentralization principles
- Gives people control over online presence, identity, and data
- Focuses on building real tools, governance models, and economic systems
- Features include:
  - Meta-communities (persistent, decentralized spaces across web)
  - On-page interactions
  - Personal data vaults
  - Federated identity
  - Context-aware governance
  - Transparent AI
  - Data sovereignty
  - AI containment strategies
  - Decentralized reputation systems
  - Content provenance
  - Transparent sourcing
  - Decentralized verification

Key Problems Solved:
1. Current Web Issues:
   - Centralized platforms control identity
   - AI manipulates information flows
   - Users lack data control
   - Social media controls visibility and engagement
   - Fragmented and platform-controlled interactions

2. Solutions:
   - Trust-driven layer for identity and context
   - Privacy-preserving data sovereignty
   - AI transparency and ethical constraints
   - Community-driven governance
   - Cross-platform meta-communities
   - Reputation based on authentic participation
   - Traceable and verifiable information
